### 🔸 Problem Solved  
**15. 3Sum**

---

### 🔸 Problem Summary  
Given an array of integers, find **all unique triplets** such that the sum of the three numbers is `0`.  
Duplicate triplets are not allowed.

---

### 🔸 Approach Used — Sorting + Two Pointers
- Sorted the array
- Fixed one element at a time
- Used two pointers to find pairs that sum to the remaining target
- Skipped duplicate values to avoid repeated triplets

---

### 🔸 Key Concepts Practiced
- Sorting-based optimization
- Two-pointer technique
- Duplicate handling
- Nested iteration with pruning

---

### 🔸 Time & Space Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** `O(n²)`
- **Space Complexity:** `O(1)` (excluding output)

---

### 🔸 Pattern Used
- Sorting + Two Pointers
- Duplicate skipping pattern
