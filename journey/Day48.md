### 🔸 Problem Solved  
**Middle of the Linked List**

---

### 🔸 Problem Summary  
Given the head of a singly linked list, return the **middle node**.  
If there are **two middle nodes**, return the **second middle node**.

---

### 🔸 Approach Used — Fast & Slow Pointer
- Used two pointers:
  - `slow` moves one step at a time
  - `fast` moves two steps at a time
- When `fast` reaches the end, `slow` points to the middle
- Naturally returns the **second middle** in case of even-length lists

---

### 🔸 Key Concepts Practiced
- Linked list traversal
- Fast & slow pointer (Tortoise–Hare technique)
- Pointer-based logic without extra space

---

- **Space Complexity:** `O(1)`
