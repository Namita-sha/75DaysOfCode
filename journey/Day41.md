### 🔸 Two Pointer Technique
Studied **3 variations** of the Two Pointer pattern:

1️⃣ **Opposite Direction Pointers**
- One pointer at start, one at end
- Used in sorted arrays (e.g., pair sum problems)

2️⃣ **Same Direction / Slow–Fast Pointers**
- One pointer moves faster than the other
- Used for removing elements, detecting cycles

3️⃣ **Window-Based Two Pointers**
- Used to maintain a valid range
- Common in sliding window problems
