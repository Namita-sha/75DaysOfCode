## 🧠 DSA Progress

### 🔸 Problem Solved  
**229. Majority Element II**

---

### 🔸 Problem Summary  
Given an integer array of size `n`, find all elements that appear **more than ⌊ n/3 ⌋ times**.  
At most **two elements** can satisfy this condition.

---

### 🔸 Approach Used — Moore’s Voting Algorithm (Extended)
- Used two candidate variables and two counters
- First pass:
  - Identified potential candidates
- Second pass:
  - Verified actual frequencies

---

### 🔸 Key Concepts Practiced
- Moore’s Voting Algorithm (generalized)
- Frequency threshold logic
- Space-optimized counting
- Understanding why only two candidates are possible

---

### 🔸 Time & Space Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** `O(n)`
- **Space Complexity:** `O(1)`
